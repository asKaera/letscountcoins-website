Let's Count Coins – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short: Let's Count Coins is a game for children that learns to count money. It does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names or photos.
  • No ads and no analytics or tracking.
  • Settings stay on your device.
  • Optional tips are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, behind a parental check.

Who we are

Let's Count Coins ("the app") is developed by an independent developer, Natalia Visla ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. It applies to the app on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts and never asks a child (or anyone) for a name, email address, phone number, photo, location or other personal details. The app contains no advertising and no analytics, tracking or social-media tools, and it does not use device advertising identifiers.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your choices between games, the app saves a few settings on the device itself: the selected currency, language, background color, sound on/off and which hints are shown. This information never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

The app reads the device's language and region settings once, on the device, to suggest a matching language and currency. This is not sent anywhere.

The pig's voice uses the text-to-speech engine built into the device's operating system to read hints aloud. The app only passes it the text of the hint.

Optional tips (in-app purchases)

Grown-ups can choose to leave an optional tip to support the app. Tips are located in a "For Parents" area that can only be opened after an adult-level check (typing numbers that are written as words), so children are not shown purchase options.

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see or receive card numbers, billing addresses or other payment details. The store tells the app only that a purchase succeeded, so it can say thank you; this is not stored by us. A tip does not unlock anything and is not required to use the app.

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

Internet access

The app works offline. It uses the internet only to communicate with Google Play or the App Store when a grown-up opens the tips screen or makes a purchase. The app does not contact any server operated by us.

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent or share any personal information with anyone.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR) and its provisions for children, Canada's PIPEDA, Mexico's data-protection law, and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All settings can be removed by deleting the app. For questions about purchases, please contact Google Play or the Apple App Store.

Coin images

United States coin images are from the United States Mint. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the United States Mint.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect. We will not start collecting personal information from children without first obtaining verifiable parental consent as the law requires.
